How to Navigate the Station

Three simple steps to find facilities and reach your train platform smoothly.

Select Locations

Pick your starting point and destination from the dropdown, or click directly on any facility marker on the interactive station map.

Toggle Accessibility

Turn on Accessibility Mode to automatically filter out all stairs and receive routes utilizing certified gentle ramps and elevators only.

Follow Guidance

Follow the animated path on the map and read the step-by-step directions. Use the voice button to listen to directions hands-free.
